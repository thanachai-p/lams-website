ABOUT FACULTY & CAMPUS

Faculty of Liberal Arts and Management Sciences

Southern Thailand's distinguished center of higher education at Prince of Songkla University, Surat Thani Campus. Dedicated to developing entrepreneurial mindsets, sustainable business innovation, and global communication proficiency.

Faculty Vision

"To be a premier faculty integrating business management and language sciences, fostering creative innovation and producing globally competent graduates responsive to the sustainable development of Southern Thailand and the international community."

Faculty Mission

  • Outcome-Based Education (OBE) integrated with hands-on corporate partnerships.
  • High-impact research and business innovation enhancing regional and national competitiveness.
  • Academic outreach, continuous professional upskilling, and executive reskilling.
