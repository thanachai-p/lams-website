Scholarships & Student Financial Aid

Faculty of Liberal Arts and Management Sciences, PSU Surat Thani

1. PSU Academic Excellence & Merit Grants

Annual tuition waivers and financial stipends awarded to students with distinguished academic achievements and talents.

2. Student Loan Fund (Thailand Student Loan)

Low-interest government student loan coverage up to 60,000–70,000 THB/year, covering tuition fees and monthly living allowances.

3. Alumni & Corporate Partner Grants

Special emergency funds and internship allowances sponsored by prominent business networks across Southern Thailand.
