Official Admissions Notice Undergraduate & Graduate Portals

How to Apply to LAMS PSU (Academic Year 2026)

Official student admissions for both Undergraduate Degrees and Master of Business Administration (MBA) are centrally administered through the Prince of Songkla University centralized admissions portals (currently operated in Thai).

1 Choose Program

Review curriculum structure, philosophy, credits, and career paths on our Programs Catalog.

2 Prepare Documents

Prepare official transcripts, national ID / passport, and language certificates (for International tracks).

3 Submit Online

Submit your application directly through the official central university portals linked below.

Apply Undergraduate (Central Thai Portal) Apply MBA (Graduate School Portal) Contact Admissions Officer

Scholarships & Student Financial Aid

Faculty of Liberal Arts and Management Sciences, PSU Surat Thani

1. PSU Academic Excellence & Merit Grants

Annual tuition waivers and financial stipends awarded to students with distinguished academic achievements and talents.

2. Student Loan Fund (Thailand Student Loan)

Low-interest government student loan coverage up to 60,000–70,000 THB/year, covering tuition fees and monthly living allowances.

3. Alumni & Corporate Partner Grants

Special emergency funds and internship allowances sponsored by prominent business networks across Southern Thailand.
